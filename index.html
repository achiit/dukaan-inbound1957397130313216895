Meet Sanju Gaikwad.

Owner, Pranav Glow Sign

I started this workshop in Labour Colony in 2008 with one table and a lot of patience. Back then a good board meant hand-painted letters. Today it means LEDs, backlit faces and clean vinyl, but my rule has not changed: the board must look right on day one and still look right years later.

Many of my customers are neighbours and shopkeepers I have known for years. I talk to every customer myself, measure the shopfront myself and check the work before it goes up.
